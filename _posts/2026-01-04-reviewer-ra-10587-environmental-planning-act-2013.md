## RA 10587: Environmental Planning Act of 2013

### Key Provisions Summary

| Provision Category | Key Details |
| :----------------- | :--- |
| **Republic Act** | RA 10587, the "Environmental Planning Act of 2013". |
| **Date Enacted** | June 12, 2013. |
| **Primary Objective** | To regulate the practice of environmental planning and professionalize the planning profession in the Philippines. |
| **Governing Body** | Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) through the Professional Regulatory Board for Environmental Planning (PRB-EP). |
| **Scope of Practice** | Includes formulation of plans and policies on land use, development, housing, environmental management, disaster risk reduction, and other related fields. |
| **Professional Title** | Registered Environmental Planner (REP). |
| **Requirements for Registration** | - Filipino citizen.<br>- Good moral character.<br>- Bachelor's degree in Environmental Planning or related field.<br>- Completion of at least one (1) year of diversified experience in environmental planning.<br>- Pass the licensure examination for environmental planners. |
| **Licensure Examination** | Administered by the PRB-EP under the PRC. Conducted annually or as needed. |
| **Foreign Reciprocity** | Allowed, subject to international agreements and PRC/PRB-EP rules. |
| **Issuance of Certificates** | Certificate of Registration and Professional Identification Card issued by PRC upon registration. |
| **Seal of Professional** | Standardized seal for registered environmental planners. |
| **Integrity of the Profession** | Prohibits illegal practice, use of fictitious names, misrepresentation, etc. |
| **Penalties** | Fines and/or imprisonment for violations. Revocation of license. |
| **Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR)** | Promulgated by the PRC and the Professional Regulatory Board for Environmental Planning (PRB-EP). (e.g., PRC Resolution No. 2014-838).

### Professional Regulatory Board for Environmental Planning (PRB-EP)

*   **Composition:** Chairman and two (2) members. All must be Filipino citizens, validly registered and licensed Environmental Planners. At least one must have experience in academe or research, and at least one in government or private practice.<br>*   **Mandate:** Administer and enforce RA 10587, develop syllabi, prepare and grade exams, issue Certificates of Registration, investigate violations.<br>

### Practice of Environmental Planning

*   **Includes:** Preparation of Comprehensive Land Use Plans (CLUPs), physical framework plans, development plans, zoning ordinances, environmental impact assessments (EIAs), disaster risk reduction and management plans, and other planning-related documents.<br>*   **Requirement:** Only registered and licensed Environmental Planners (REPs) can sign and seal these documents. Individual practitioners must be REPs. Juridical entities must employ REPs for their planning services.<br>
